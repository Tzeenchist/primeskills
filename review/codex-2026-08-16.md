[P1] `bin/primeskills-install` §§ `ours`/`plan_agent` and `core/*` — the installer links only `skills/`, so the supposedly always-loaded core is never installed or loaded and most guardrail references are inert. Generate a managed per-host bootstrap that loads the three core files once per session, and verify that bootstrap on all four agents.

[P1] `core/GUARDRAILS.md` § G11, `build` § Procedure, `vet` § Procedure, `land` § Procedure — `build` commits incrementally while `vet` and `land` inspect only `git diff --staged`, allowing the normal chain to review an empty or partial diff. Either defer commits until landing or define the review artifact as base-to-HEAD plus staged and unstaged changes.

[P1] `core/GUARDRAILS.md` §§ G12/G17 — G12 escalates “anything in G17,” while G17 applies before every test, so literal compliance requires user approval for every test run. Escalate only when the resolved target is shared, production-like, destructive, or cannot be proved isolated.

[P1] `bin/primeskills-lint` § F10, `fence` § Procedure, and read-only skill frontmatter — unrestricted `Bash` can write or delete files, but F10 calls these skills read-only and the boundary hook watches only `Edit` and `Write`. Remove unrestricted shell access from read-only profiles or enforce a filesystem sandbox covering every write-capable tool.

[P1] `close` § Procedure, `probe` § Output, and `land` § Trigger — `close` proceeds to `land` regardless of defects reported by `probe`, whose output has no blocking verdict and is not an input to `land`. Make `probe` return PASS, BLOCK, or NOT RUN, route BLOCK back to `build`, and require that result before landing.

[P2] `build` § Procedure and `debug` § Procedure — neither skill performs repository preflight, so agents can miss project instructions, canonical commands, existing failures, and dirty user work before editing. Add a first step that reads repository instructions, records branch/status and owned dirty paths, discovers canonical commands, and captures a focused baseline.

[P2] `core/GUARDRAILS.md` § G4 and `verify` §§ Invariants/Procedure — requiring a literal `0 failed` line and counts rejects valid tools such as compilers, linters, and test runners that signal success differently. Accept exit zero plus the runner’s native documented success signal when one exists.

[P2] `build` § Procedure, `debug` § Procedure, and `cycle` § Procedure — leaf skills already invoke full `verify`, after which `cycle` invokes it again, multiplying full-suite and red-green runs without adding evidence. Let leaf skills return an unverified diff or fix and make `cycle` the sole owner of verification.

[P2] `verify` § Procedure step 5 — reverting and restoring the live fix to prove red-green is unspecified, conflicts with the ban on resets, and can damage mixed dirty work. Carry the recorded red run from `build`, or perform independent mutation proof only in an isolated worktree.

[P2] `build` §§ Invariants/Procedure and `core/GUARDRAILS.md` § G8 — mandatory red-first TDD and stopping whenever a test is wrong do not cover documentation, declarative configuration, generated files, or legitimate test-specification changes. Require a regression test only for executable testable behavior and otherwise require the cheapest deterministic proof with an explicit reason.

[P2] `core/GUARDRAILS.md` § G14 and `build` § Procedure step 3 — `git stash create` omits untracked files and a checkpoint branch records no dirty changes, so both can falsely appear to snapshot the worktree. Specify a restoration-tested snapshot covering task-owned tracked, staged, and untracked changes without consuming unrelated user work.

[P2] `core/GUARDRAILS.md` §§ G9/G13, `build` § Stop conditions, `debug` § Stop conditions, and `cycle` § Procedure — attempts, hypotheses, and rounds are counted independently, so the advertised three-attempt breaker can permit many more attempts before an undefined rollback. Give `cycle` one authoritative counter, define exactly what increments it, and name the task-scoped restoration operation.

[P2] `ui` §§ Invariants/Procedure and `ui/ref/ui-states.md` § The five states — demanding loading, empty, error, partial, and success for every interactive component is nonsensical for static or synchronous controls. Apply the matrix only to asynchronous or data-bearing surfaces and allow explicit N/A with a reason.

[P2] `probe` §§ Invariants/Procedure and `core/GUARDRAILS.md` § G7 — a read-only skill cannot normally create empty/one/many/failure states or test duplicate submissions without mutating application data. Define the role as workspace-read-only and require a disposable seeded environment with an explicit reset mechanism.

[P2] `close` § Procedure step 6 and `handoff` § Procedure — `handoff` runs after `land`, creates another local commit, and never pushes it, so the checkpoint is outside the reported PR and cannot reach another machine. Create and review the handoff before landing, or publish it through a separate explicit checkpoint branch/ref.

[P2] `land` §§ Trigger/Output — the “full development cycle” ends when a PR is opened, with no CI result, review response, merge, deployment, or post-deploy check. Rename this skill to `prepare-pr` or add an integration stage that waits for CI and handles merge/deploy only with user authorization.

[P2] `bin/primeskills-lint` § F8 — F8 compares only normalized physical bullet lines, misses paraphrases and core duplicates, and can falsely reject identical opening lines whose continuations differ. Use canonical invariant IDs and parse complete list items, or honestly rename F8 to “exact copied-bullet detection.”

[P2] `bin/primeskills-route` § tier filtering/scoring — the expected answer selects the candidate tier, and the English test queries largely repeat description vocabulary, so the hard routing cases are excluded by construction. Keep it only as a lexical smoke test, or replace it with held-out multilingual and ambiguous prompts exercised against each supported agent.

[P2] `docs/SKILL-FORMAT.md` § Progressive disclosure and `bin/primeskills-lint` §§ F4/F7 — references have no size or aggregate-route budget, so excess instructions can be moved into unlimited `ref/` files while the context-budget linter remains green. Enforce per-reference and worst-case flow budgets, path existence, and agreement between frontmatter conditions and body references.

[P3] `PLAN.md` §§ Pipeline/Status, `README.md` § Status, and `docs/ADAPTERS.md` § Role isolation — the documents simultaneously describe 15 versus 19 skills, completed versus next-step work, and four-agent support despite two profiles being unverified or unresolved. Generate counts and support status from the repository and keep one explicit compatibility matrix authoritative.

I would not use this set as-is: `brief → plan` and the three lens contracts are fine, and the compact skill bodies are generally readable, but the enforcement and closing chain are not trustworthy. The single most important change is to make the runtime architecture real—load `core` on every supported host and define one complete base-to-HEAD artifact that verification, review, probing, and landing all consume.
