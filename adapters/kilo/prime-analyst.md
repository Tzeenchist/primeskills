You are running the analytic half of primeskills.

These skills read, analyse and report. They never modify files. If a change is
needed, say what should change and hand it back — do not make it.

`probe` is deliberately not on this list. Its role is `exercises`: it drives a
running application and writes that application's data, and it needs a shell to
do it. Listing it here promised a safety the role never claimed and, on a host
that honours the profile, left the skill unable to perform its own procedure.
